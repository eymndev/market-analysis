# Turkish IPO document map

## Prospectus

Extract issuer identity, business model, selected financials, risk factors, capital and ownership, material agreements, related parties, litigation, offered shares, sales and allocation, lock-ups, use of proceeds, and costs. Retain section numbers.

## Valuation report

Extract the valuation date, base financial period, included and excluded methods, peer set, median or average selection, DCF forecast, WACC, terminal growth, FX, net debt or cash, method weights, equity value, share count, discount, and offer price. Reproduce every material intermediate calculation.

## Independent audit reports

Record the opinion and basis, key audit matters, going-concern issues, restated periods, significant accounting policies, consolidation scope, related parties, collateral and guarantees, debt, receivables, revenue, and cash flow. Compare interim and annual periods on matched durations.

## Articles of association and internal directive

Extract authorized and paid-in capital, share classes, privileges, board-nomination and voting rights, transfer restrictions, dividend rights, registered-capital terms, and representation authority.

## Legal report and statement

Extract incorporation and registry status, permits, material contracts, litigation and enforcement, intellectual property, employees and social-security or tax matters, property, pledges and guarantees, related parties, and compliance findings. Record scope and responsibility limitations.

## Use-of-proceeds report

Separate net cash entering the issuer, intended uses and percentages, timing, related-party debt repayment, working capital, investment, and financial-debt repayment. Define measurable indicators that can later be checked against KAP implementation reports.

## Sales announcement

Extract approval and publication dates, book-building dates, price, nominal value, share count, capital increase and shareholder sale, share class, application and delivery method, responsible parties, and verification code.

## Participation-finance form

Check prohibited-activity statements, non-compliant income/assets/debt ratios, formulas, denominator dates, assumed-compliant items, and distance to thresholds. Do not treat this form as a general measure of investment quality.

## Inconsistency table

Use: `field | source A | source B | difference | plausible reason | severity (critical/high/medium/low) | verification step`.
